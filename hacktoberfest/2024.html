Kick-off with Minsk Python Meetup, 2024

After a couple of quieter years on the community calendar, Hacktoberfest returned to our Initiatives list with an MLH-listed online kick-off on the first evening of October — a chance to prepare together, contribute to open-source projects, and collaborate with like-minded people.

  • When: Tuesday, 1 October 2024, 19:30–21:30 UTC+3
  • Format: online
  • Listed with: MLH
  • Audience: professionals, students, ages 13+

What we covered

The session was practical and contributor-focused. The published agenda highlighted three themes:

  • how to get started with Hacktoberfest;
  • tips for contributing to Python-related open-source projects;
  • best practices for submitting quality pull requests.

In other words: less ceremony, more “how do I find a good issue and open a PR maintainers will actually want.”

That year’s challenge

Hacktoberfest 2024 kept the quality-first model that emerged after 2020. Participants registered on the official site, then aimed for four high-quality pull or merge requests between 1 and 31 October on GitHub or GitLab projects that had opted in (typically via the hacktoberfest topic). Maintainers had to accept those contributions for them to count. Registration unlocked a digital Holopin badge featuring mascot Sloan the Sloth, which leveled up as PRs were accepted.

DigitalOcean’s how to participate guide also spelled out maintainer prep: topic tags, good first issue / hacktoberfest labels, a clear CONTRIBUTING.md, and rejecting spam with the right labels.
