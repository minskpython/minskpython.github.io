Hacktoberfest kick-off, 2021

Like the year before, Minsk Python Meetup wanted to back the global open-source push in October — and do “irreparable good” for whichever projects we could reach. The 2021 edition opened with a Friday evening online meetup in Discord to talk plans for the month.

  • When: Friday, 1 October 2021, 20:00
  • Where: Discord
  • Format: online community meetup

Who was invited

Everyone interested was welcome, regardless of programming experience. Language of code did not matter — though Python was especially encouraged. The call explicitly opened the door to:

  • beginners writing their first contributions;
  • developers leveling up their open-source practice;
  • people from other IT specialties curious about the topic;
  • creators and maintainers of open-source projects and repositories;
  • people already active in open-source communities.

The meetup itself was a place to sync: share what you hoped to work on in October, ask how registration and rules worked that year, and find company for the month ahead.

That year’s rules (the post-2020 reset)

After the spam-heavy chaos of 2020, Hacktoberfest 2021 leaned hard into maintainer-friendly defaults. Repositories on GitHub and — for the first time in a major way — GitLab needed to opt in (for example with a hacktoberfest topic). Only accepted pull or merge requests counted toward the challenge. Maintainers could earn recognition without submitting PRs themselves. Completing four accepted contributions remained the classic path to the limited-edition shirt, while smaller participation (even a single PR or attending an event) was still encouraged.

Official details lived on the Hacktoberfest site (then under the DigitalOcean umbrella). DigitalOcean also published a 2021 announcement covering GitLab support and the maintainer-focused rule changes.
